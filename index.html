          How it works

          Same rules every day

          How to read a call

          • BUYPrice closed outside the opening range. Buy the option named, near the price shown.
          • HOLDYou're in. Take a third off at each of the next two pivots. The stop line is the exit.
          • SELLA target (sell a third) or an exit. The label says which, and the option percent, so a stock stop at entry is not shown as a flat trade.
          • WAITNo signal yet. Nothing to do until a five-minute close leaves the box.
          • NO TRADENo break, or a reason to pass. Sitting out is part of the plan.
          9:30–9:45

          Draw the box

          The first three five-minute candles make a box: the high and the low of the opening fifteen minutes. No trades before it's done.

          After 9:45

          Wait for a close outside

          A five-minute candle has to close above or below the box, with volume picking up. A poke that closes back inside doesn't count. Last entry is 11:00.

          In the trade

          Take profit in pieces

          A third off at each of the next two pivot levels, the rest out by 3:30. After the first sale the stop moves to the stock's entry price. That is not break-even on the option. The option can still finish down.

          If it's wrong

          Get out fast

          A five-minute close back inside the box ends the trade. A hard −50% on the option backs that up. The chart names the exit by what it did to the option.

          Days I sit out

          • Monthly options expiration
          • Fed decision days
          • CPI and PPI mornings
          • No momentum on the break
          • Fridays: small bets only

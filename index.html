    How it works

    Same rules every day

    How to read a call

    • BUYPrice closed outside the opening range. Buy the option named, at about the price shown.
    • HOLDYou're in. Take profit in thirds at the targets. The "Sell all if" line is your exit.
    • SELLA target hit (sell a third) or an exit rule hit (sell everything). Do it, no second-guessing.
    • WAITNo signal yet. The buy levels are listed. Nothing to do until one breaks.
    • NO TRADENo break, or a reason to pass. Sitting out is part of the plan.
    9:30–9:45

    Draw the box

    The first three five-minute candles make a box: the high and low of the opening fifteen minutes. No trades before it's done.

    After 9:45

    Wait for a close outside

    A five-minute candle has to close above or below the box, with volume picking up. A quick poke that closes back inside doesn't count.

    In the trade

    Take profit in pieces

    A third off at +20%, most of it gone by +75%. After the first sale the stop moves to break-even, so a winner can't turn into a loser.

    If it's wrong

    Get out fast

    A five-minute close back inside the box ends the trade. These are same-day options, so a hard −50% stop backs that up.

    Days I sit out

    • Monthly options expiration
    • Fed decision days
    • CPI and PPI mornings
    • A box that's too wide
    • No momentum on the break
    • Fridays: small bets only
